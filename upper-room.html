The Upper Room

The Upper Room Prayer facility — set apart for prayer, for our region, our nation, and the world.

A Place for Prayer

The Upper Room is a dedicated facility set apart for prayer. It includes the building, a prayer walk, campsites, and the surrounding grounds — a quiet place to seek the Lord.

A Place of Prayer

It is our desire that this be a house of prayer for all of God's people — for night and day, around-the-clock prayer. We long to see pastors and churches join together in prayer, and to hold prayer events that lift up:

  • Mifflin County and the surrounding counties
  • Pennsylvania — the Keystone State
  • The exercise of spiritual authority in our area
  • Our nation and its leaders
  • The nations of the world
  • All people who are bound, afflicted, or tormented

Teaching & Preaching on Prayer

Alongside prayer itself, we are committed to promoting a Bible-based knowledge of prayer — teaching and preaching that equips God's people to pray with understanding and faith.

"…for mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people." — Isaiah 56:7
