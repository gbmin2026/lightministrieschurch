Our Church Family

About Us

Light Ministries Church welcomes you! We are the family of God — a community-minded, non-denominational, Spirit-filled, Bible-believing church that loves Jesus and one another, and holds to the message of faith in God's Word.

Choosing a church is an important step in life. Young, old, single, married, divorced, single parent — there is a place for you in the family! It is our sincere desire to see you grow in your walk with God, and your family as well.

If you are looking for a church with loving people, welcoming people, vibrant, faith-filled praise and worship, clear and relevant teaching of God's Word, and meaningful and fun teaching for children and youth, you will find it with us here at Light Ministries Church.

Whether you are living in, looking to move in, or just passing through the Lewistown area — stop in, visit, and hear what God has for YOU!

We are the Church of the Living God, the pillar and ground of the Truth — preaching the things concerning the kingdom of God, and the name of Jesus Christ.

Our Heart

We are a family church that loves Jesus and one another. Our emphasis is worship, teaching, prayer, fellowship, and outreach. Pastor Gary Bailey and his wife Gwen would be honored to welcome you.

Service Times

Join us for worship every Sunday at 10:00 AM at 181 Caldwell Hill Road, Lewistown, PA. You are also welcome to join our livestream service on Facebook at Light Ministries Church.
