Keep on the Sunny Side of Life

By Pastor Gary Bailey · Light Ministries Church, Lewistown, PA

There is an old song made famous by the Carter Family called “Keep on the Sunny Side.” The words are simple, but they carry a truth many people need today. The song says there is a dark and troubled side of life, but there is also a bright and sunny side too.

That does not mean life is always easy. It does not mean we pretend trouble is not real. Every person knows something about cloudy days. Families face pressure. People carry grief. Bills come due. Bodies get tired. Hearts get heavy. Life in this world can bring storms we did not expect and burdens we did not choose.

But even when we cannot choose every circumstance, we still have choices to make. We can choose how we respond. We can choose where we place our trust. We can choose whether we will walk toward light or darkness, hope or despair, good or evil, faith or unbelief.

The Bible puts this choice before us very plainly. Moses said, “I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life” (Deuteronomy 30:19). That is not complicated, but it is deeply important. Every day, in words, attitudes, decisions, relationships, and habits, we are choosing a direction.

Some choices look small at the time. A harsh word. A bitter attitude. A dishonest shortcut. A wrong friendship. A secret compromise. A step away from God. But small steps can lead a person down a long road. Many people do not plan to end up far from where they should be. They simply make one choice, then another, then another, until they wonder how they got where they are.

That is why we must be intentional. Proverbs says, “The path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day” (Proverbs 4:18). The next verse says, “The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble” (Proverbs 4:19). One path grows brighter. The other grows darker.

God wants us to learn the difference.

There are things in life that pull us toward darkness. Anger. Envy. Lust. Pride. Greed. Addiction. Dishonesty. Bitterness. Sin may offer pleasure for a season, but it never tells the truth about where it leads. It promises freedom and brings bondage. It promises happiness and leaves sorrow behind.

Jesus said, “I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life” (John 8:12). That is the sunny side in its truest sense. It is not merely positive thinking. It is not pretending everything is fine. It is choosing to walk with Christ, even when the day is cloudy.

The Christian life is not the absence of trouble. Psalm 23 does not say we will never walk through the valley. It says, “Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me.” The comfort is not that there is no valley. The comfort is that we do not walk through it alone.

That is why hope matters. The old song says, “Clouds and storms will in time pass away; the sun again will shine bright and clear.” A believer can say that with confidence because God is faithful. Trouble may come, but it does not have the final word. Darkness may gather, but it cannot overcome the light of God.

If you have made wrong choices, there is still hope. The mercy of God is greater than your worst day. You can turn around. You can repent. You can come home. Jesus Christ came into the world to save sinners, restore broken lives, and bring people out of darkness into light.

And if you are trying to do right but life still feels hard, do not give up. Keep choosing life. Keep choosing truth. Keep choosing the good. Keep choosing to forgive. Keep choosing to pray. Keep choosing to trust God. Keep choosing to follow Jesus.

Romans 12:9 says, “Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good.” That is good counsel for all of us. Hate what destroys. Hold tightly to what gives life.

The sunny side of life is not found by ignoring reality. It is found by walking with the Savior through reality. It is found in the light of Christ, the goodness of God, and the hope of His promises.

So today, choose life. Choose light. Choose the good. Choose Christ.

And whatever the weather looks like right now, keep on the sunny side.
